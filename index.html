Six mockups: three directions, two pages each

Design mockups for review. This is not Hamza Tzortzis’s official website, and nothing here has been approved by him. Each of the three design directions is shown as a home page and an essay page. Scroll the home pages slowly; most of the idea is in how they move.

1 · Marginalia recommended

A page with a living margin. The argument sits in the centre; sources, Arabic, dates and related work sit beside it.

Try: scroll the opening sentence; point at a marked phrase in the essay; follow “Why Love God” at the foot of the home page.

HomeEssay

2 · The Lit Window

A dark room and one window. You arrive in the dark of the question; each scroll lets in more light; reading happens in daylight.

Try: scroll from the first question through the book; move over the list of debates; switch the essay to lamplight.

HomeEssay

3 · In Question

Every page opens with the question it answers, set as the largest thing on the screen: question, answer, objection, reply.

Try: scroll each question until it shrinks to the left and its answer writes in on the right.

HomeEssay

What these are, and are not

  • Typefaces are the free stand-ins. The paid faces the plan recommends (Skolar PE, 29LT Idris, November) are not licensed yet, so each page uses the free fallback named in the plan. Expect the final type to look finer.
  • No generated images and no portrait. Light and shade are drawn in code. The only picture is the real book cover.
  • The essay is his real text (Why Love God, 2017). Titles, years, reading times and durations come from the research files. Qur’an verses were fetched from quran.com, not typed or generated.
  • Placeholder wording is marked †. The opening sentence, the choice of verse, the question wording and the short summaries are drafts for him to replace or approve. Endorsements are marked “to be re-cleared”.
  • Most links go nowhere. Only the home and essay pages exist in each direction.
  • Not the official site. Wording marked † is draft, and the endorsements are quoted from his old book page until they are re-cleared.
